1. Introduction

ZAIST ("we", "us", or "our") provides the MOSAIXA mobile game (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

By using MOSAIXA, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it before using data for personalized advertising.

2. Information processed by MOSAIXA

MOSAIXA does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, postal address, photographs, contacts, or precise location.

The App stores gameplay information locally on your device, such as unlocked puzzles, completed levels, game settings, and progress. This local information is used to operate the game and may be removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily include in your message. We use it only to respond, provide support, protect the App, or comply with legal obligations.

3. Advertising and automatically processed data

MOSAIXA displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements through Unity LevelPlay and participating advertising partners. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and applicable law, these providers may process:

  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device information such as model, operating system, language, network type, and app version;
  • Advertising interactions, impressions, clicks, reward events, and fraud-prevention signals;
  • Diagnostics, crash information, and performance data associated with advertising services.

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, provide rewarded ads, limit repeated ads, detect fraud and abuse, troubleshoot services, and—only where permitted and consented—to personalize advertising.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the App presents a consent message allowing you to accept, reject, or manage eligible advertising purposes and partners. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads; you may receive contextual or non-personalized ads instead.

You may reopen the privacy choices from the App's menu when this option is required and available. You can also reset or limit the Android Advertising ID through your device's privacy settings.

5. Legal bases

Depending on your location, data may be processed based on your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the App, performance of the service you request (including rewarded ads), and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

6. Third-party services and sharing

Advertising-related information may be processed by Unity LevelPlay and advertising partners participating in its mediation services. These providers act under their own privacy policies and may process data in countries outside your country of residence.

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Privacy Information
  • Google Privacy Policy (when Google advertising services participate)

We do not sell information that you submit directly to us. Advertising providers may use or share advertising data as described in their own policies and according to your consent choices and applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay progress remains locally on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain security records, or meet legal requirements, and are then deleted or anonymized.

Advertising providers determine retention periods for data they process. You may contact those providers directly regarding their records. You may also email us to request deletion of information you previously sent directly to us.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

MOSAIXA is intended only for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children or minors under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from anyone under 18. If you believe a child or minor has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. To exercise rights concerning information held directly by ZAIST, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your request.

11. International transfers

Advertising providers may process information on servers located outside your country. Where required, providers use recognized safeguards for international data transfers. Please review the linked provider policies for details.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, providers, or law. The current version will be published at this location with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated inside the App where appropriate.

3. الإعلانات والبيانات المعالجة تلقائيًا

تعرض MOSAIXA إعلانات بانر وإعلانات بينية وإعلانات بمكافأة من خلال Unity LevelPlay وشركائها الإعلانيين. وبحسب الجهاز والمنطقة وخيارات الموافقة والقانون، قد يعالج هؤلاء المزودون:

  • معرّفات الإعلانات، ومنها Android Advertising ID؛
  • عنوان IP والموقع التقريبي المستنتج منه؛
  • نوع الجهاز ونظام التشغيل واللغة ونوع الشبكة وإصدار التطبيق؛
  • مرات ظهور الإعلانات والنقرات والتفاعل وأحداث المكافأة وإشارات منع الاحتيال؛
  • بيانات الأعطال والتشخيص والأداء المرتبطة بخدمات الإعلان.

قد تستخدم هذه المعلومات لعرض الإعلانات وقياسها، وتقديم المكافآت، وتقليل تكرار الإعلان، ومنع الاحتيال، وحل المشكلات، ولتخصيص الإعلان فقط عندما يسمح القانون بذلك وبعد الحصول على الموافقة المطلوبة.

عند الحاجة، بما في ذلك داخل المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية والمملكة المتحدة، يعرض التطبيق رسالة موافقة تتيح القبول أو الرفض أو إدارة أغراض الإعلان والشركاء المؤهلين. رفض الإعلانات المخصصة لا يعني بالضرورة إزالة الإعلانات، فقد تظهر إعلانات سياقية أو غير مخصصة.

يمكنك إعادة فتح خيارات الخصوصية من قائمة التطبيق عندما يكون هذا الخيار مطلوبًا ومتاحًا. ويمكنك كذلك إعادة تعيين معرّف إعلانات Android أو تقييده من إعدادات خصوصية الجهاز.

5. الأسس القانونية

بحسب بلدك، تستند المعالجة إلى موافقتك، أو مصالحنا المشروعة في تشغيل التطبيق وحمايته، أو تنفيذ الخدمة التي تطلبها ومنها الإعلان بمكافأة، أو الالتزام بالقانون. يمكنك سحب الموافقة في أي وقت من دون التأثير في المعالجة التي تمت قبل السحب.

قد تعالج Unity LevelPlay وشركات الإعلان المشاركة في خدمات الوساطة معلومات متعلقة بالإعلانات. تعمل هذه الجهات وفق سياسات الخصوصية الخاصة بها، وقد تعالج البيانات خارج بلد إقامتك.
